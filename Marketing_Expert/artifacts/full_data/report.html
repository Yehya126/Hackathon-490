Coffee shop suggestions

Trained on all 51,177 lines across 33,887 transactions. These are observed buying patterns, not validated predictions. Former validation and test transactions are included; future independent evaluation needs new transactions.

How we separate suggestions: Product/category priority requires at least 30 purchases together, 5% observed confidence and an association ratio of 1.10 (10% above individual popularity). Tentative requires at least 10 purchases and a ratio above 1. Otherwise evidence is weak. Product suggestions have positive original-price gross profit.

For dine-in, priority requires at least 50 product orders, a majority dine-in, and a dine-in share 5 percentage points above the branch baseline. Tentative requires at least 20 orders, a majority dine-in and any positive advantage. These are adjustable review heuristics, not significance tests or proven profitability. All tiers are visible by default.

Orders/month is the historical average over 6 calendar months (Jan 2026–Jun 2026), including months with no matching orders. Dine-in rows count only dine-in purchases. This is not a monthly forecast. Prices/profits use historical median original unit prices/costs, one of each product, without discounts. Within tiers, combinations rank by frequency × profit; categories by frequency; dine-in by advantage over the baseline.

Share of multi-item orders: percentage of orders containing the combination out of all orders with at least two distinct products in the selected branch. Both combination tables use this same denominator, including orders with multiple products from one category. Orders containing extra products also count; rows overlap, so percentages need not add to 100%.
