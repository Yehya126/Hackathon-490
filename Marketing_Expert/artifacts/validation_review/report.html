Combination validation review

Exploratory candidates, not approved recommendations. Ranked within each scope using training support × gross profit. One unit of each product; historical median original prices and costs, without discounts. No test data was used.

What qualifies for inclusion?

Product and category combinations: at least 20 purchases together in EACH period; bought together at least 20% more often than expected from individual popularity in BOTH periods; at least 5 in 100 orders with the first item also include the second in BOTH periods. Purchase rate and that conditional percentage must each retain at least 75% of their earlier level. Product combinations must also have positive original-price gross profit.

Dine-in products: at least 50 orders containing the product in EACH period, more than half dine-in, and a dine-in share at least 10 percentage points above the relevant branch's usual share in BOTH periods.

These conservative starting rules define an owner-review shortlist, not proven business value. They are not significance tests and were introduced after exploring validation results; the untouched test period remains necessary for a final independent check. An empty shortlist is an acceptable result. Use “Show rejected candidates” to inspect failures.

The tables below include all branches and an Overall view. Earlier orders found the patterns; later orders check whether they held up. Different period sizes mean purchase rates are more comparable than raw counts. These are buying associations, not proof one purchase causes another. Category patterns concern distinct categories only.
